# Crafting Tomorrow

**Curriculum intelligence for a changing world.**

Crafting Tomorrow builds the infrastructure that keeps education in step with reality. Our Curriculum Intelligence System and Analytical Engine monitor how labour markets and technology evolve and surface where curricula and L&S activities need to catch up.

## What we build

- **JanusEd** — our curriculum intelligence platform for schools and education authorities
- **ParentBites** — helping families navigate AI literacy and digital parenting → [parentbites.eu](https://parentbites.eu)

## How we build

**EU-sovereign by design.** Our infrastructure runs on EU-incorporated, EU-controlled providers. Data stays in Europe not as a compliance checkbox, but as an architectural principle. GDPR-first, cookieless analytics, no exceptions.
